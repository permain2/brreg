# Comprehensive Annual Health Screening Plan

**Profile:** 30 years old | Based in Portugal | Budget: €2,000/year

---

## Tier 1 — Core Blood Panel (Essential, ~€300–500)

### Complete Blood Count (CBC / Hemograma completo)
- Red blood cells, white blood cells, platelets, hemoglobin, hematocrit
- Detects: anemia, infections, blood disorders

### Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP)
- Glucose (fasting)
- HbA1c (glycated hemoglobin) — 3-month blood sugar average
- BUN / Creatinine — kidney function
- eGFR — kidney filtration rate
- ALT, AST, GGT, ALP — liver enzymes
- Bilirubin (total + direct)
- Albumin / Total protein
- Sodium, Potassium, Chloride, Calcium, Magnesium, Phosphorus — electrolytes

### Lipid Panel (Full)
- Total cholesterol
- LDL cholesterol
- HDL cholesterol
- Triglycerides
- VLDL
- ApoB (apolipoprotein B) — **better CVD risk predictor than LDL alone**
- Lp(a) (lipoprotein(a)) — **only need to test ONCE in your life** (genetic, doesn't change)

### Iron Panel
- Serum iron
- Ferritin
- TIBC (total iron-binding capacity)
- Transferrin saturation

---

## Tier 2 — Hormones & Thyroid (~€200–400)

### Thyroid Panel
- TSH
- Free T3
- Free T4
- Thyroid antibodies (anti-TPO, anti-Tg) — test once, repeat if abnormal

### Male Hormones (if male)
- Total testosterone
- Free testosterone
- SHBG (sex hormone-binding globulin)
- Estradiol (E2)
- DHEA-S
- Prolactin

### Female Hormones (if female)
- Estradiol
- Progesterone (day 21 of cycle)
- FSH / LH
- DHEA-S
- Prolactin
- AMH (anti-Müllerian hormone) — ovarian reserve, test once in your 30s

### Both Sexes
- Cortisol (morning, fasting)
- Insulin (fasting) — early insulin resistance detection
- HOMA-IR (calculated from fasting glucose + insulin)

---

## Tier 3 — Vitamins & Micronutrients (~€150–300)

- Vitamin D (25-OH) — **extremely common deficiency even in Portugal**
- Vitamin B12
- Folate (B9)
- Homocysteine — cardiovascular + B-vitamin status marker
- Zinc
- Selenium
- Omega-3 Index (if available) — red blood cell EPA+DHA ratio
- Copper (optional)
- RBC Magnesium (more accurate than serum magnesium, if available)

---

## Tier 4 — Inflammation & Immune Markers (~€100–200)

- hs-CRP (high-sensitivity C-reactive protein) — systemic inflammation
- ESR (erythrocyte sedimentation rate)
- Uric acid — gout risk + metabolic health
- Fibrinogen
- Immunoglobulins (IgA, IgG, IgM) — optional, baseline immune function

---

## Tier 5 — Cancer Screening Markers (~€50–100)

> Note: Tumor markers are NOT diagnostic on their own. They establish a baseline.

- PSA (prostate-specific antigen) — **if male**, baseline at 30 is useful
- CEA (carcinoembryonic antigen) — optional baseline
- AFP (alpha-fetoprotein) — optional liver baseline

---

## Tier 6 — Infectious Disease Screening (~€100–200)

*One-time baseline (repeat only if exposure risk changes):*

- HIV 1/2 (antibody + antigen)
- Hepatitis B (HBsAg, anti-HBs, anti-HBc)
- Hepatitis C (anti-HCV)
- Syphilis (RPR/VDRL)
- CMV IgG/IgM
- EBV panel (if never tested)
- H. pylori (stool antigen or breath test) — stomach ulcer/cancer risk

---

## Tier 7 — Urinalysis & Stool (~€50–100)

- Complete urinalysis (protein, glucose, blood, pH, specific gravity)
- Stool occult blood test (FOBT/FIT) — early colorectal screening
- Stool calprotectin — intestinal inflammation marker (optional)

---

## Tier 8 — Advanced / Specialized Tests (~€200–400)

### Cardiovascular Deep Dive
- Coronary Artery Calcium (CAC) Score — **CT scan, not blood test** (~€100–200)
  - Gold standard for early heart disease detection at 30
  - Only need every 3–5 years if score is 0

### Genetic / One-Time Tests
- Lp(a) — (listed above, only once)
- MTHFR gene variant — affects folate metabolism (optional)
- ApoE genotype — Alzheimer's + cardiovascular risk (optional, consider implications)

### Other Imaging (if budget allows)
- Abdominal ultrasound — liver, kidneys, spleen, gallbladder (~€50–100)
- Thyroid ultrasound — if thyroid antibodies are positive (~€50–80)

## Where to Get These in Portugal

### Option A — Private Labs in Portugal (Best Value)
- **Germano de Sousa** — large network, comprehensive panels
- **Joaquim Chaves Saúde** — well-equipped, good pricing
- **Unilabs Portugal** — international chain
- **CUF** — private hospital network with lab services
- **Hospital da Luz** — full executive check-up packages

> Most Portuguese private labs offer "check-up executivo" packages (€200–600) that cover many items above. You can then add individual tests à la carte.

### Option B — Cross-Border (Spain)
- Not usually necessary — Portugal's private lab pricing is competitive
- Spanish labs like **Synlab** or **Quirónsalud** are options if you're near the border

### Option C — Online / Mail-Order Blood Tests
- **Medichecks** (UK, ships kits to Portugal)
- **Lets Get Checked** (ships to EU)
- These are good for specific hard-to-find tests like Omega-3 Index

## Non-Blood Tests to Add (within budget)

These complete your full-body check-up:

1. **Blood pressure measurement** — free at any pharmacy in Portugal
2. **Resting ECG (electrocardiogram)** — ~€30–50, baseline heart rhythm
3. **Eye exam (ophthalmology)** — ~€50–80, every 2 years
4. **Dermatology full-body skin check** — ~€50–80, yearly (mole mapping)
5. **Dental check-up + cleaning** — ~€50–100, every 6 months
6. **Hearing test (audiometry)** — ~€30–50, baseline at 30

---

## Practical Tips

1. **Fast 12–14 hours** before blood draw (water is fine)
2. **Morning draw** (before 10am) — hormones like testosterone and cortisol peak in the morning
3. **Avoid intense exercise 48h before** — skews CRP, liver enzymes, CK
4. **Bring previous results** to track trends over time
5. **Ask for digital copies** of all results (PDF) — build your own health record
6. **Schedule the same month each year** for consistency
7. **If any marker is abnormal**, your GP can refer you through SNS (Portuguese public health system) for follow-up at no/low cost
